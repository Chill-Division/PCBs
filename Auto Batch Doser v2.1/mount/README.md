# Auto Batch Doser v2.1 wall mount

Open-air standoff brackets for the two short ends of the 488.95 x 74.14 mm
SystemV1 board — deliberately not an enclosure: the board is longer than a
256 mm printer bed and the stepper drivers carry heatsinks that need airflow.
Generated by `abd_mount.py` from data parsed out of `SystemV1.PcbDoc`.

## Files

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `ABD-Mount-Left.stl`  | Bracket for the board's left end (one boss reaches 23.7 mm inboard — the lower-left M3 hole is not at the corner) |
| `ABD-Mount-Right.stl` | Bracket for the right end (vertical pair of holes) |
| `ABD-Mount-Mid.stl`   | Mid-span support clip at x = 251 (between stepper channels 3 and 4) |
| `abd_mount.py`        | Parametric generator (`pip install manifold3d numpy`) |
| `render_preview.py`   | Regenerates the preview PNGs |

## Design notes

- The PCB sits on Ø8 bosses **12 mm off the wall** (`STANDOFF_H`), fixed by
  M3 self-tapping screws into Ø2.6 pilots through the board's four 3 mm holes.
- Stiffening webs stop 2.5 mm below the board so trimmed through-hole leads
  never touch the bracket.
- Each bracket has **two ears extending lengthwise past the board end**
  (Ø4.5 holes for M4/#8 wall screws), so the wall screws are driven clear of
  the PCB. Total wall footprint is ~518 mm end to end.
- The two brackets are different parts — the left end's lower hole is offset
  inboard on the PCB, so the left bracket has an L-shaped web reaching it.
- **Mid-span clip**: the driver heatsinks overhang the bottom board edge on
  the component side, so the clip is asymmetric. It hooks rigidly over the
  TOP edge (bare mid-board) and supports the bottom edge from behind with a
  ledge that stays below board level, passing under the fin overhang -
  nothing on the clip rises above the board on the heatsink side. It sits in
  the component-free gap between channels 3 and 4 (x = 236..266, verified
  free of parts and through-hole joints on both edge strips); `CLIP_X` moves
  it. To fit: tilt it, hook the top edge, swing the bottom ledge under, then
  wall-screw the ears. The bottom ear is extra long so its screw lands
  ~25 mm below the board edge, clear of the fins.

## Hardware

- 4x M3 knurled brass heat-set inserts (4 mm, 4.5 mm OD) — PCB bosses
- 4x M3 machine screws (M3 x 6–8) — PCB to bosses
- 6x M4 (or #8) screws + anchors to suit the wall (4 end ears + 2 mid clip)

## Printing

All three parts print flat as oriented, no supports. PETG recommended for a
warm environment; 4 perimeters. Everything fits a 256 x 256 bed in one go
(end brackets ~80 x 42 mm, mid clip ~34 x 123 mm).

## Assembly

1. Fit the mid clip to the bare board first: hook the top edge, swing the
   bottom ledge under.
2. Screw both end brackets to the wall 405.5 mm apart: with the left bracket's
   ear holes at x = -7, the right bracket's are at x = +496 relative to the
   board's left edge (the alignment PNG shows exact geometry). Easiest is to
   screw the brackets to the PCB first, hold the assembly level against the
   wall, and mark all four wall holes through the ears.
3. Drive the four M3 screws through the PCB holes into the bosses, then
   mark and drive the mid clip's two wall screws through its ears.
